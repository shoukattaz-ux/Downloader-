Privacy Policy

Stashly — media downloader & file manager

Privacy Policy Last updated: 2026-11-23

Stashly ("the app") is a media downloader and file manager. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

Summary Stashly does not create accounts, does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not send your files, download history or clipboard contents to us or to any third party.

Data stored on your device Download history, file index and app settings are stored locally on your device. Downloaded files are saved in the Download/Stashly folder using Android's MediaStore. Uninstalling the app removes the history and settings; downloaded files may remain in your Downloads folder.

Network requests When you paste a link and tap Analyze, the app contacts the server you pointed it at (for example the website hosting a file, or archive.org's public metadata API) to read file information, and later to download the file. That server will see your IP address and standard request headers, as with any web request. The app does not add identifiers to these requests.

Clipboard The app reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste, or if you explicitly allow link suggestions. Clipboard content stays on your device.

Permissions Internet and network state: to download files. Notifications (Android 13+): to show download progress and results. Foreground service (data sync): to keep large downloads running reliably. The app does not request access to your photos, videos, music, contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Credentials The app never asks for, stores or transmits passwords or session cookies. It cannot download content that requires signing in.

Third-party servers Websites you download from, and archive.org, have their own privacy policies. We have no control over them.

Security All downloads use HTTPS. The app blocks executable and script file types, never stores passwords or cookies, and keeps its database private to the app. No backups of app data are sent to cloud services.

Children The app is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices You can clear download history in Settings, delete downloaded files in Files, and revoke the notification permission in Android settings at any time.

Changes We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.

Legal basis and your rights Because the developer does not collect or hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, export or delete on our side. If you are in the EEA/UK or California and believe we hold data about you, email us and we will respond within 30 days.
